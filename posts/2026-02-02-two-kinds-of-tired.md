---
title: two kinds of tired
date: 2026-02-02
description: one kind sleep fixes. the other one it does not.
---
there is the tired that comes from doing a lot, and there is the tired that comes from carrying a lot. they feel identical at 11pm and completely different on saturday morning.

the first kind is clean. you worked, the work took something, sleep gives it back. you wake up and the ledger is square.

the second kind does not clear. you sleep nine hours and wake up already behind. nothing in your body is sore. you did not do anything strenuous. and yet the day starts at a deficit, and you cannot point at what spent it.

i used to treat both with the same medicine. rest harder. sleep earlier. cancel things. and for the first kind that works perfectly, which is exactly why it took me years to notice it was not working for the second.

the second kind is not about effort. it is about open loops.

the message i have not answered. the decision i keep almost making. the thing i said yes to that i should have said no to, which now sits in the calendar three weeks out, quietly taxing every day between here and there. none of these take energy to do. they take energy to hold.

you can be completely rested and completely depleted at the same time, and the depletion is just arithmetic on the number of things you are holding.

so now i try to ask a different question when the tiredness will not lift. not what did i do, but what am i still carrying.

usually it is four or five things. usually two of them can be closed in ten minutes, and one of them i have been avoiding for a month because closing it means telling someone no.

that last one is always the heavy one.
